# Monster Game Character Guide

Names powers weaknesses and matchups

Five giant monsters form a playable roster with different ways to fight. This guide records their names, visual identities, strengths, weaknesses, signature moves, and favorable matchups. The gameplay rules are proposals to tune through playtesting.

## Roster at a glance

| Monster | Combat role | Signature move |
| --- | --- | --- |
| KORVAK | Heavy close-range bruiser | Magma Quake |
| VELUMORA | Fast aerial attacker | Thunder Vortex |
| ORUVEX | Ranged fighter with water cannons | Tidal Siege |
| CANOPYX | Defender with roots and regeneration | Forest Lock |
| PRISMOR | Agile melee fighter and trickster | Prism Reversal |

## KORVAK

**The Walking Volcano**

**Visual identity:** A six-legged volcanic beast with charcoal basalt armor, glowing orange cracks, and large geothermal shoulder vents.

**Combat role:** Heavy close-range bruiser.

### Strengths

- Highest armor and close-range damage in the roster.
- Heavy attacks resist interruption.
- Ground slams damage several nearby enemies.

### Weaknesses

- Slow movement and turning make attacks easier to dodge.
- Water attacks temporarily cool and crack its armor.
- Glowing shoulder vents become vulnerable after a powerful attack.

### Signature move

**Magma Quake.** Slams its front limbs down, sending three expanding shockwaves across the ground.

**Favorable matchup:** CANOPYX. Heat burns its defenses and interrupts healing.

**Counter to watch:** ORUVEX.

## VELUMORA

**The Storm Hunter**

**Visual identity:** A purple and silver flying predator with broad curled crescent wings, electric membranes, and three long ribbon tails.

**Combat role:** Fast aerial attacker.

### Strengths

- Fastest monster in the roster.
- Can fly over ground hazards and attack from above.
- Fires lightning at distant enemies.

### Weaknesses

- Low health and fragile wing membranes.
- Powerful water jets can briefly force it to land.
- Must land periodically to recover flight stamina.

### Signature move

**Thunder Vortex.** Creates a moving cyclone that pulls enemies toward its center before releasing a lightning strike.

**Favorable matchup:** ORUVEX. Electricity exploits its exposed pressure sacs.

**Counter to watch:** PRISMOR.

## ORUVEX

**The Tidebreaker**

**Visual identity:** A pearl-white and turquoise ocean creature with three legs, coral-pink gills, visible pressure sacs, and two siphon arms.

**Combat role:** Ranged fighter with water cannons.

### Strengths

- Longest sustained attack range in the roster.
- Water cannons push opponents away, interrupt charges, and cool heated armor.
- Its three-legged stance resists knockback.

### Weaknesses

- Slow sideways movement.
- Glowing pressure sacs are vulnerable to electricity.
- An empty water reserve creates a refill window when its cannons cannot fire.

### Signature move

**Tidal Siege.** Anchors all three feet and unleashes two powerful water streams. It cannot move while firing.

**Favorable matchup:** KORVAK. Water weakens its armor and stops its charges.

**Counter to watch:** VELUMORA.

## CANOPYX

**The Living Fortress**

**Visual identity:** A mobile forest guardian with a seed-pod body, a glowing golden core, a broad leaf canopy, and five articulated root limbs.

**Combat role:** Defender with roots and regeneration.

### Strengths

- Grabs and immobilizes enemies with roots.
- Creates wooden barriers.
- Slowly regenerates health after avoiding damage for several seconds.

### Weaknesses

- Fire burns away its barriers and temporarily stops regeneration.
- Root attacks have a visible wind-up.
- Attacking with several roots at once exposes its golden core.

### Signature move

**Forest Lock.** Roots erupt around a selected area, trapping grounded enemies for a short time.

**Favorable matchup:** PRISMOR. Roots bypass its reflective shield and restrict dodging.

**Counter to watch:** KORVAK.

## PRISMOR

**The Mirror Gladiator**

**Visual identity:** A cobalt and iridescent crystal fighter with three arms, two powerful legs, a vertical glowing face slit, and a round prism shield.

**Combat role:** Agile melee fighter and trickster.

### Strengths

- Quick dodges and strong melee combinations.
- A precisely timed directional shield block reflects energy projectiles.
- Can create a short-lived visual decoy.

### Weaknesses

- Moderate health; its shield protects only the front and consumes energy.
- Cannot reflect physical strikes, roots, shockwaves, or water streams.
- Missed heavy punches leave it exposed.

### Signature move

**Prism Reversal.** Briefly raises its shield. A successful energy reflection powers up its next punch.

**Favorable matchup:** VELUMORA. Well-timed blocks send lightning back at the flyer.

**Counter to watch:** CANOPYX.

## Matchups and game balance

Each monster has one intended favorable matchup and one main counter. Other interactions still matter: Oruvex can force Velumora to land, for example, even though Velumora has the favorable overall matchup.

### Favorable matchups

| Monster | Favored opponent | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| KORVAK | CANOPYX | Heat burns its defenses and interrupts healing. |
| VELUMORA | ORUVEX | Electricity exploits its exposed pressure sacs. |
| ORUVEX | KORVAK | Water weakens its armor and stops its charges. |
| CANOPYX | PRISMOR | Roots bypass its reflective shield and restrict dodging. |
| PRISMOR | VELUMORA | Well-timed blocks send lightning back at the flyer. |

### Rules for fair battles

**Keep matchups winnable.** Treat favorable matchups as advantages rather than automatic victories. Timing, movement, and resource management should let a skilled player win with any monster.

**Make powerful attacks readable.** Give each powerful attack a visible warning and a short recovery window. Opponents should have a chance to dodge, interrupt, or punish it.

**Make resources visible.** Clearly show flight stamina, water reserves, shield energy, and regeneration status. Players should understand why an ability is temporarily unavailable.

**Tune after playtesting.** Adjust health, damage, movement speed, ability duration, and cooldowns after testing. Check that one monster or one repeatable attack does not dominate every battle.
